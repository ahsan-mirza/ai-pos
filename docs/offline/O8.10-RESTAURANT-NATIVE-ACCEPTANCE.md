The business-wide owner `o810owner` called `PUT /api/v1/settings/profile` with the Restaurant preset. The response profile was `restaurant` and the capability list included `open_orders`. Tenancy stayed off on this SQLite API, the same shape as the grocery acceptance API. A store-bound user was not used to change the business type.

## Native activation

The activation screen accepted the Downtown store code and the one-time key. The success panel showed:

- Business: O810 Restaurant
- Store code: o810
- Branch: O810 · Downtown Restaurant
- Register: DTA · Downtown Till A

After restart the unlock screen listed Downtown Cashier, Downtown Manager, and O810 Owner under `O810 RESTAURANT · DOWNTOWN RESTAURANT · POS REGISTER`. The cashier unlock opened a shift for Downtown Restaurant.

## Menu and ticket

The menu screen showed Dine-in, Takeaway, and Delivery. The catalogue card was category MAINS, name O810 Grill, price USD 18.00. The local `products` row is `O810 Grill`, SKU `O810-GRILL`, `price_cents` 1800. `business_settings.profile_json` has `business_profile` `restaurant` and `open_orders` among 10 capabilities.

`register_menu` and `register_floor` each have 0 rows. The product is in `products` and `menu_cache` has one row whose body does not contain the grill name. The Downtown floor and the Marina floor were created on the API. They are not in the local floor table.

A takeaway ticket was started for customer Walkup. The header read `Takeaway - Order #3E55` and `Takeaway ticket is open. Add dishes below.` Adding O810 Grill opened a quantity dialog. The quantity field became 11 before Add to sale. The cart then showed `O810 Grill added.`, 11 items, and USD 207.90. That total is 11 × 18.00 plus tax. The modifier group `O810 Doneness` exists on the server and was not attached to the product, so the quantity dialog did not ask for a modifier.

The status bar stayed `ACTIVATED`, `OFFLINE`, `SETUP INCOMPLETE`, `1 PRODUCT`. The local `sync_queue` row seen before the ticket was `register_shift` with status `synced`. The takeaway line was not settled.
