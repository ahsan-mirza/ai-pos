# Offline bootstrap

Status: not implemented.

The register already caches products, cashiers who have unlocked online, and preferences. It does not download a checked snapshot of tax, receipts, allocations, laundry services, or restaurant menus, and it has no Offline Ready gate.

`OFFLINE READY` must not be shown until activation, device binding, the local database, catalogue, pricing, tax, users, permissions, the business profile, an allocation where the profile needs one, and the queue check have all passed. That screen does not exist.
