# Laundry product scope

## This delivery

A laundry business can be selected at signup or in business settings. An administrator can turn on one or more operating modes, apply a configuration template, and maintain service categories, services, and price lists. A quote uses the business tax configuration and refuses an expired, wrong-branch, or wrong-customer price list.

Templates:

- Small shop: wash and fold, wash and iron, pressing. Bag tracking.
- Dry cleaner: dry cleaning, pressing, alterations. Individual tracking.
- Multi-branch: wash and fold, dry cleaning, pickup and delivery, central plant. Individual tracking.
- Commercial: commercial laundry and wash and fold. Bulk tracking.

Applying a template inserts missing categories, garment types, three starter services, and three workflow definitions. It does not replace a service the business already added. Starter rates are zero until a price is entered, and a zero rate is refused on a quote unless that service explicitly allows it.

## Later phases, not in this delivery

Counter intake, tags, photos, production, payments against a laundry order, collection, delivery, packages, corporate contracts, machine maintenance, consumable consumption, and reports. Those stay on the existing customer, tax, payment, invoice, and inventory services when they are built.
