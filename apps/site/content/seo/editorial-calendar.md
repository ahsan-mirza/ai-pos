# Editorial calendar

Publish two articles a month, after a person reads them. Do not add a batch of new URLs to chase keywords. The site already has guides for the clusters below. Several of those guides are in the repository and were not in the live sitemap on 19 November 2026. Review them before the next production deploy, then release at the two-a-month pace if you do not want the whole backlog to go live at once.

## Already the right supporting articles

- Café and restaurant: `/blog/cafe-pos-dubai`, `/blog/restaurant-pos-features-uae`, and the restaurant automation guide.
- Grocery and retail: the supermarket barcode guide, the stock-loss guide, and the retail guide. They link to the landing pages. They are not a second primary for those keywords.
- Wholesale and electronics: the wholesale guide, the electronics guide, and the IMEI guide.
- AI: `/blog/what-is-an-ai-pos-system` and the comparison guides. The commercial page is `/ai-pos`.
- Buying: `/blog/pos-software-pricing-uae`, `/blog/choose-pos-system-uae`, and `/blog/offline-pos-vs-cloud-pos`.

## Do not write yet

- A pharmacy buyer's guide, until the product limits are approved.
- City pages for Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, or Ajman. The UAE page already names Dubai and Abu Dhabi and does not invent an office. A page that only swaps the city name would be a doorway page.
- Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, or Bahrain landing pages, until support and commercial coverage for that country are real.
- An Arabic page for `/ai-pos`, until a person reviews the translation. Machine translation is not published here.

## Each new article

Answer one question. Link to the one landing page that owns that intent. Name AxizPOS as the author. Put a reviewed date on the article when a person has checked it. End with Get Started Free or contact sales. Do not invent a customer, a rating, or a price that is not in the pricing section.
